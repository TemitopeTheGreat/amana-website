Your Career Path with Amana

Through the Amana Academy, you can develop practical skills, earn certifications and progress through a structured career ladder.

1

Trainee

You join the Amana talent pool and complete role-specific training and assessment at the Amana Academy.

2

Certified Professional

Once trained and vetted, you're certified and matched to households looking for your role and experience level.

3

Senior Professional

With a track record of reliable placements, you take on higher-experience roles and more specialised households.

4

Lead / House Manager

Experienced professionals can progress into overseeing other staff, vendors and day-to-day household operations.

How to Join the Amana Talent Pool

A clear, structured process — from application to your first placement.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Tell us about your experience, the role you're looking for and your availability.

  2. 2

    Get verified

    We run identity, guarantor, reference and medical checks, plus a role-specific assessment.

  3. 3

    Train at the Academy

    Complete practical training covering the skills and standards your role requires.

  4. 4

    Get matched

    We introduce you to households whose needs, schedule and location fit your profile.

  5. 5

    Receive ongoing supervision, refresher training and support as you progress in your career.

What You'll Learn at the Amana Academy

Practical, role-specific training designed to meet the standards modern homes expect.

  • Household hygiene and safety
  • Childcare and basic first aid
  • Food handling and nutrition basics
  • Professional conduct
  • Confidentiality
  • Financial honesty and handling household funds
  • Defensive driving and vehicle care
  • Customer service and household etiquette

You can also receive refresher training as you continue working with Amana.
