The Amana Academy

Training professionals for the standards modern homes expect. Being available for work isn't the same as being prepared for professional household work. The Amana Academy provides practical, role-specific training before placement.

  • Household hygiene and safety
  • Childcare and basic first aid
  • Food handling and nutrition basics
  • Professional conduct
  • Confidentiality
  • Financial honesty and handling household funds
  • Defensive driving and vehicle care
  • Customer service and household etiquette

Professionals can also receive refresher training as they continue working with Amana.
